Clima apps

Climate analysis and design guidance for the early stage of architectural projects. Everything runs in the browser. Nothing leaves it unless you use the optional AI assistant with your own API key.

Macro Clima

Reads a weather file: comfort, sun, wind, future climate and urban heat island.

Micro Clima

Site and street scale: outdoor comfort, shading, surfaces and heat island.

Room Clima

Room and facade: searches window size, glazing, shading, ventilation, mass and fans against daylight, comfort, energy and whole-life carbon targets, with the DIN 4108-2 summer check.
Giovanni Betti. Code under MIT, documentation and validation under CC BY 4.0. Source, validation and notes: github.com/gvnbetti/clima-apps. Bundled weather: TMYx (Lawrie and Crawley, climate.onebuilding.org) and TMY3 (NREL).
