Validation

Every model in Clima Toolbox is checked against an established simulation tool, an open reference implementation, a published test or an exact solution. The checks compare models with models: they show that each tool computes what it claims and agrees with the tools the field relies on. They do not compare the tools with measured buildings or outdoor spaces.

Each report in the repository gives the base cases, the assumptions, the reference and the scripts, so every result can be rerun.

Known limits

  • Future climate: the shipped table differs from the reconstructed calculation by 0.05 K on average and up to about 0.5 K in single months; monthly mean shifts are up to 0.18 K off after morphing; sky infrared rises by 10.4 W/m² where pyepwmorph gives 12.8 W/m². Corrections are under review.
  • Micro Clima computes the urban heat island with its own sun position: up to 0.77 K apart from Macro Clima in at most 2.2 % of hours, around sunrise and sunset.
  • Room Clima's hourly model is tuned to its own rooms: in the Standard 140 test box it reaches 32 of 44 ranges and overestimates cooling by 12.7 %.
  • Not yet checked independently: the Givoni-Milne strategy zones of Macro Clima, and the German national annex comfort band (DIN EN 16798-1/NA), for which no open reference exists.
  • Daylight blinds are modelled as a diffusing layer; the angle dependence of venetian slats is not modelled.
