Clima Map: method

What it is for

Finding a typical-year weather file for a site. Clima Map computes nothing about the climate itself; it helps choose the station whose climate is closest to the site's, then hands the file to the other apps.

How it works

  • Stations. The catalogue of Climate.OneBuilding.org and the EnergyPlus weather collection, as used in Macro Clima (macro/data/stations.js): 29,787 stations and 67,686 files. Entries of the same station listed under several source folders are merged: same name, within about 2 km.
  • Climate type. The Köppen-Geiger class given in the catalogue for each station.
  • Nearest stations. Great-circle distance from the chosen point. Elevation is shown so that a station with similar surroundings can be preferred.
  • Place search. OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service, only on request (Enter), as its usage policy requires.
  • Files. Downloaded directly from their source as ZIP files. The other apps read the ZIP as downloaded.
  • Map. OpenStreetMap tiles, no account or key.

Assumptions

  • The nearest station is not always the best one. Elevation, distance to the coast and the station's surroundings (airport, city, valley) matter as much as distance. The map shows them; the choice is the user's.
  • Where a station has several files (TMYx periods, TMY3, IWEC, national sets), the most recent TMYx period is the usual choice: it is built from the most recent years.

Limits

  • The catalogue was read in 2026 and does not include the newest TMYx periods for every station.
  • A typical year contains typical weather, not extremes. Heat waves and cold spells are under-represented by construction.

Validation

No calculation to check. The station list is the source catalogue, merged as described.
