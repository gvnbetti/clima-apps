Room Clima: how carbon is calculated

Room Clima compares designs by whole-life carbon over 50 years, per m² of floor. It counts only what the options change: the embodied carbon of the elements a design adds or removes, with their replacements, and the operational carbon of the energy the room uses. The structure, floors, partitions and services that every design shares are left out. The figures are therefore for comparing designs of the same room, not a building LCA.

Code: carbonOf in room/src/core.js. Values: the carbon.* entries of room/src/register.js. All are generic assumptions, to be replaced by project data or the Build-up LCA datasets (ÖKOBAUDAT).

Formula

Whole-life carbon = embodied carbon of the options + delivered energy × grid factor × 50 years

  • Study period: 50 years (BNB and DGNB reference study period).
  • Replacements: an element with a service life L is counted ⌈50 / L⌉ times.
  • Operational carbon: delivered energy (all electric, through a heat pump) × 0.25 kg CO₂e/kWh × 50. The factor is an assumed mean over 50 years; the German mix was about 0.38 kg/kWh in 2024 (UBA) and is set to fall. It can be changed in the app.

Embodied carbon of the options

Element Value Life Measured on

Interior textile blind 6 kg CO₂e/m² 15 years window area
Overhang, fins, frame 90 kg CO₂e per m² of projected element 50 years depth × length (overhang over the facade width, two fins over the window height)
Thermal mass, against light construction medium 15, heavy 45, very heavy 60 kg CO₂e per m² of floor 50 years floor area
Ceiling fan 35 kg CO₂e per fan, one per 12 m² of floor 15 years number of fans
Night ventilation, tilt restrictors and actuators 15 kg CO₂e per facade 20 years facade
Night ventilation across storeys 5 kg CO₂e per m² of floor 20 years floor area

The window area is the window-to-wall ratio times the facade. A corner room counts both facades. The thermal mass values are the least certain of the set.

Worked example

Office, one facade, 3.6 × 5.4 m, 3.0 m high (19.44 m² floor, 10.8 m² facade). Computed with the current code.

Element Design A: 50 % window, triple, exterior blind, 0.5 m overhang, medium mass, night tilt, fans Design B: 30 % window, double, interior blind, heavy mass
Window 5.4 m² × 75 × 2 = 810 kg → 41.7 kg/m² 3.24 m² × 55 × 2 = 356 kg → 18.3 kg/m²
Wall 5.4 m² × (45 + 0.42 × 24) = 297 kg → 15.3 7.56 m² × (45 + 0.42 × 14) = 385 kg → 19.8
Blinds 5.4 m² × 40 × 2 = 432 kg → 22.2 3.24 m² × 6 × 4 = 78 kg → 4.0
Overhang 90 × 0.5 × 3.6 = 162 kg → 8.3 0
Thermal mass 15.0 45.0
Fans 35 × 2 × 4 = 280 kg → 14.4 0
Night ventilation 15 × 3 = 45 kg → 2.3 0
Embodied, per m² floor 119.2 kg CO₂e/m² 87.1 kg CO₂e/m²

Operational carbon adds 12.5 kg CO₂e/m² for each kWh/m²a of delivered energy (0.25 × 50). At 10 kWh/m²a that is 125 kg CO₂e/m²: of the same order as the embodied part. A design that saves 2 kWh/m²a each year therefore justifies about 25 kg CO₂e/m² more embodied carbon.

How carbon enters the recommendation

Carbon is not a target and not part of the score. It decides between designs that are otherwise equal: among designs within 0.01 in score and in the same tier, the one with less whole-life carbon is recommended. After the main search, a local search looks for the design with the least whole-life carbon among those that meet every target, because carbon is not one of the search criteria and the search alone could miss it.
